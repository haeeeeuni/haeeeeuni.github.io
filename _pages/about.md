I am an M.S. student in Management Information Systems at the Graduate School of Business, Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Wooje Cho. My research examines how digital platforms shape consumer behavior and how firms' digital activities relate to their performance and risk, using econometric and machine learning methods.

Before joining SNU, I earned a B.E. in Software Convergence and a B.B.A. in Business Administration from Sookmyung Women's University, with an exchange semester in computer science at the University of Bern.
